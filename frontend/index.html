Methodology

The model simulates a bank open for a fixed number of minutes. Each minute, a customer arrives with probability p; arrivals wait in a first-in, first-out queue and are served by the first free counter. Each service lasts a random 2–8 minutes.

Arrival probability (p)
The per-minute chance that a new customer walks in. Higher p means a busier bank and more pressure on the queue.
Number of counters (k)
How many customers can be served in parallel. Each counter handles one customer at a time.
Average waiting time
The mean time, in minutes, that served customers spend queueing before service begins. A customer reaching an idle counter waits zero minutes.
Why more counters help
Additional counters raise the rate at which the queue is drained. Once the combined service rate exceeds the arrival rate, the queue stops growing and waiting time falls sharply—visible in the charts above.
